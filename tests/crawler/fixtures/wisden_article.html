SA20 2027 auction, live updates: Full list of players sold and unsold, remaining purse and updated squads

Oct 07, 2026 3 minute read News
Share

The SA20 2027 auction will see a total of 156 players go under the hammer. Here are all the latest updates from the auction.

SA20 2027 auction, live updates

(Auction to start at 5:10pm local time (8:40pm IST, 4:10pm UK time)

The player auction for the 2027 edition of SA20 will begin shortly, as a total of 156 players are set to go under the hammer, with 95 of them being from South Africa and 61 from other countries. A maximum of 19 slots are to fill at the auction with Faf du Plessis, Nandre Burger, Trent Boult, Shimron Hetmyer, Mohammad Nabi, and Colin Munro among many others expected to trigger a bidding war.

Joburg Super Kings and MI Cape Town are two teams with maximum slots in the team (6 each), while Pretoria Capitals and Sunrisers Eastern Cape have five and two slots to fill, respectively in their squads. Paarl Royals and Durban Super Giants won't be attending the auction as they have all their slots filled.

The player auction for SA20 2027, the fifth edition of the tournament, will witness a tussle between four franchises for the 156 shortlisted players, with Paarl Royals and Durban's Super Giants already fulfilling their squad requirements.

Each team has a total purse of R42 million to assemble their squad for the next season, which begins on January 17.

How the teams stack up ahead of the SA20 2027 auction

Each team can have a maximum of 19 players in their squad, including a Wildcard and two mandatory U23 players. Joburg Super Kings and MI Cape Town have six slots to fill, while Pretoria Capitals and Sunrisers Eastern Cape have five and two remaining slots, respectively.

Durban Super Giants

Aiden Markram, Andile Simelane, Daryn Dupavillon, David Bedingham, Devon Conway, Eathan Bosch, Evan Jones, Gerald Coetzee, Heinrich Klaasen (Wildcard), Jos Buttler*, Kane Williamson*, Kwena Maphaka, Kyle Simmonds, Liam Livingstone*, Marques Ackerman, Sam Curran*, Simon Harmer, Sunil Narine*, Tony de Zorzi.

Joburg Super Kings

MI Cape Town

Remaining purse for each team
